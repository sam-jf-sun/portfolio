Enabling informal traders with mobile business management

2022
Project Overview

For informal traders who balance business demands with daily life, losing track of stock means lost revenue and constant worry. Sizamina gives these entrepreneurs secure, mobile access to their inventory, enabling them to monitor stock levels, make informed decisions and run their businesses confidently from anywhere.

Required Output

A live deployed mobile app across 5 pilot outlets in Johannesburg.

Problem Statement

Informal traders lack real-time stock visibility, preventing informed business decisions while balancing daily responsibilities. Without accessible, affordable management tools designed for their context, they struggle to control inventory and optimise operations. Solving this is critical to improve their income stability, reduce waste and enable business growth while maintaining work-life balance.

07

Development

Working within tight deadlines and scope, I created mockups based on user feedback and requirements. The design maintained Touchsides' brand identity while creating a distinct new product. The regular collaboration with front-end developers ensured designs could be built within our timeline. As team lead, I kept the team focused through retrospectives, backlog sessions and prioritisation meetings. It was also important for me to set clear success metrics so we could measure whether the final product met user needs and business goals in the end.

Sizamina development mockups
08

The Launch

We successfully deployed our app across 5 outlets within 18 weeks. We continued to engage with tavern owners, absorbing their insights and conducting user tests. The user feedback sentiment was overall very positive. One month later, with the assistance of Google Analytics, we began to measure its success, tracking how regularly our tavern owners accessed the app and where they navigated to. The results showed enough positive data for the business to increase deployment to 50 outlets.
