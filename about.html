About Resume Let's Talk

Hi,

Happy you're here!

From a young age, I've been driven by a constant need to explore and master. My school years were filled with creative pursuits like violin and orchestra and competitive activities such as squash and basketball. I was always looking for something new to learn and challenge me.

As a professional architect, I contributed to 14+ projects, from commercial and hospitality skyscrapers to educational buildings and an international project in France, focusing on the rigorous process of designing, planning and executing high-rise structures. I enjoyed translating complex blueprints into tangible, functional structures. But eventually, I felt the familiar itch for a new challenge.

My attention pivoted to the tech industry, prompting a fundamental question: "Is building a mobile app like designing a physical space? Do they share the same strategic DNA of planning and user-focused execution?" Driven by this, I began my transition into product design.

Now, I leverage my architectural foundation to solve meaningful problems, while continuing to refine my craft with the same passion that first drew me to it.

AI and me

AI is embedded across every phase of my process now, not as a novelty but as a part of how I work. The shift is that it has compressed the time between having an idea and having something tangible to react to. Although I work closely with AI, there are still things that remain in my hands, such as taste, craft and judgement.
