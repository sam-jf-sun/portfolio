Cognia

Restructuring the platform to better serve district leaders

2026
Project Overview

District leaders, responsible for overseeing every school within their district, need a streamlined way to gain visibility into the overall health of their district while easily exploring individual schools to identify areas of concern. The redesigned experience should provide clear comparisons against district-wide goals, enabling leaders to quickly identify trends, prioritise actions and make informed decisions.

Required Output

A PowerPoint presentation showing the future vision that Cognia plans to implement in phases over the next 18 months.

Problem Statement

District leaders lack a centralised view of how schools are progressing against their KPIs. With data spread across multiple areas of the platform, it is difficult to compare schools and understand the overall health of the district, limiting confidence in district-wide decision making.

01

Gathering Requirements

We received a 40-page document outlining the brief, detailed requirements and the user journeys the client wanted to see. As a starting point, I manually translated the document into FigJam screens, which made the vision and desired layout far clearer.

Translating the requirements brief into structured FigJam screens
02

Mapping the Discovery Journey

I noticed the journey they'd mapped out closely mirrored the principles already shown on their public website. That connection made it clear those values needed to stay embedded in the new design.

Mapping the discovery journey against Cognia's existing principles
03

Exploring Initial Concepts

I prompted Claude to help generate concepts, asking it to think not from a digital product or UX perspective, but through narrative and storytelling, while still keeping Cognia's values in mind.

Narrative direction 1 - Vantage point
Narrative direction 2 - Weather forecast
Narrative direction 3 - The Loom and Thread
04

Gathering Concept Feedback

Presenting the concepts to the client surfaced constructive feedback on each direction. We invited them to collaborate and ideate with us, keeping them closely involved throughout the discovery process. While these concepts weren't validated directly with district leaders given the 1-month timeline, the Cognia stakeholders are subject matter experts in the education industry themselves, so we relied on their deep domain knowledge to steer the direction.

Collaborative feedback session with Cognia stakeholders on the three chosen directions
05

Building the Moodboard

Once the client helped narrow down the concept, we built a moodboard for inspiration, drawing not just from educational platforms but from any platform that sparked ideas relevant to the concept.

Moodboard drawing inspiration from beyond education platforms
06

From Low Fidelity to Final Designs

Starting with quick low fidelity mockups, I began shaping the concept, introducing components and standards more familiar to a digital product. Once translated into high fidelity, the solution felt less like a standard UI and more like something novel and creative, built around Cognia's story.

07

Bringing the Concept to Life

After the final designs were complete, I built an interactive prototype to bring the concept to life, letting stakeholders click through the experience as a district leader would rather than just view static screens.

Interactive prototype showing stakeholders the experience as a district leader
08

Positive Reception and Path to Development

The prototype was met with positive feedback from Cognia stakeholders and the board. It's now being introduced into development in phases, and I'm working closely with the dev team to get it shipped over the next 18 months.

"First, I just want to say how incredibly helpful this design process has been. Seeing the vision come to life in Figma and actually trying to interact with it from the perspective of a district leader has pushed my thinking in a really good way. It is helping clarify not only what the experience should look like, but how we need to structure the thinking behind it."

Vice President of the Lab, Cognia
